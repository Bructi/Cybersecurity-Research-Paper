## 1. Government and Cybersecurity Agency Sources

### 1.1 European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA)
1. **ENISA Threat Landscape 2025 (ETL 2025)**
   - **Authors/Agency:** European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA)
   - **Publication Date:** October 2025
   - **URL:** `https://www.enisa.europa.eu/publications/enisa-threat-landscape-2025`

   - **Authors/Agency:** European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA)

## 3. Academic and Peer-Reviewed Literature

15. **Not What You've Signed Up For: Compromising Real-World LLM-Integrated Applications with Indirect Prompt Injection**

## 5. Major Documented Case Studies

34. **Hong Kong Multinational Video Conference Deepfake Fraud ($25.6M USD / HK$200M)**
    - **Date:** January–February 2024
    - **Jurisdiction:** Hong Kong Police Force (Cyber Security and Technology Crime Bureau)
    - **Mechanism:** Real-time multi-person video and voice deepfake simulation during a multi-participant CFO conference call, authorizing 15 fraudulent transactions.
    - **Source:** Hong Kong Police Force Official Briefing, South China Morning Post, CNN.
    - **Status:** Verified legal/police case study.

35. **UK Energy Firm CEO AI Voice Cloning Fraud ($243,000 USD / €220,000)**
    - **Date:** March 2019
    - **Insurer/Forensics:** Euler Hermes Group SA
    - **Mechanism:** Deep learning acoustic voice cloning imitating the CEO of a German parent company requesting emergency transfer to a Hungarian supplier.
    - **Source:** Euler Hermes Official Report, Wall Street Journal.
    - **Status:** Verified forensic case study.

36. **DARPA Artificial Intelligence Cyber Challenge (AIxCC) 2024–2025**
    - **Organization:** Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA) in collaboration with ARPA-H
    - **Scope:** Competitive evaluation of Cyber Reasoning Systems (CRS) utilizing automated LLM/symbolic reasoning for autonomous vulnerability discovery and patch synthesis in critical open-source software (Linux kernel, Apache, BIND9).
    - **Status:** Verified empirical initiative.
